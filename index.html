How do I place an order? +

Visit our official Facebook page and send us a message with the product you're interested in. Our team can confirm current availability, prices, and delivery arrangements before you purchase.

Can I enjoy Ube Latte hot or iced? +

Yes. The published Deep Ube introduction describes it as suitable hot or iced. Follow the preparation instructions printed on your actual pack.

What pack sizes and prices are available? +

The published Deep Ube post describes a pack of ten 25 g sachets. Packaging artwork shows other sizes, so we recommend confirming the exact variant, net weight, quantity and current price with our team before ordering.

What are the ingredients and allergens? +

Please refer to the ingredient and allergen declaration on the physical packaging, or request the current label from our team. We do not publish unverified dietary or ingredient claims.

Do you offer reseller or wholesale enquiries? +

We welcome business enquiries. Please email 1064euroblend@gmail.com with your company and quantity requirements so the team can discuss options.
