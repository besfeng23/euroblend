04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

For current prices or product availability, please speak with the Euro Blend team directly.

How can I order?+

Please use our official Facebook page to enquire. We can confirm availability, prices, payment instructions, and delivery options before you order.

Can I make Deep Ube hot or iced?+

Yes. The published Deep Ube introduction describes both serving options. Follow the preparation directions provided with your actual pack.

Are Classic Ube and Ube Milk available?+

These names appear on the collection artwork. Please confirm product availability and formulas with Euro Blend before ordering.

What about ingredients, allergens, and weight?+

Check the official label on the product you receive or request the current specification from Euro Blend. Artwork and post quantities are not fully consistent, so we do not assume pack details.

Is Ube + Chocolate available now?+

Ube with chocolate is a flavor pairing being explored, not a confirmed item for sale. Contact Euro Blend for the current product list.

Can I enquire about wholesale or reselling?+

You can send a business enquiry to 1064euroblend@gmail.com. Terms, quantities and availability are subject to confirmation.
