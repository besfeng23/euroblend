04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

For current prices or product availability, please speak with the Euro Blend team directly.

How can I order?+

Please use our official Facebook page to enquire. We can confirm availability, prices, payment instructions, and delivery options before you order.

How do I prepare each flavor?+

The published Deep Ube introduction described the drink as suitable hot or iced. Our website features hot Ube Latte. Follow the directions on your specific product pack and confirm other flavor preparation with Euro Blend.

Which flavors are included?+

Euro Blend lists eight flavors: Ube Latte, Ube Milk, Ube Classic, Ube Macchiato, Ube Vanilla, Ube Salted Caramel, Ube Criollo Cacao, and Ube Criollo Salted Caramel. Confirm current formats and availability with the team.

What about ingredients, allergens, and weight?+

Request the current physical label for ingredients, allergens, pack weight, and quantity. Our concept packaging image shows 20 g sachets, while the Facebook post previously stated 25 g; neither has been verified as the final commercial specification.

Can I enquire about wholesale or reselling?+

You can send a business enquiry to 1064euroblend@gmail.com. Terms, quantities and availability are subject to confirmation.
