How can I order?+

Please use our official Facebook page to enquire. We can confirm availability, prices, payment instructions, and delivery options before you order.

Can I make Deep Ube hot or iced?+

Yes. The published Deep Ube introduction describes both serving options. Follow the preparation directions provided with your actual pack.
