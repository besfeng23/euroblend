05 / THE DETAILS

Good things
to know.

For the latest availability, prices, packaging information and preparation guidance, our team can help.

What are the eight Euro Blend flavors?+

Ube Macchiato, Ube Criollo, Ube Criollo Salted Caramel, Ube Salted Caramel, Ube Vanilla, Ube Classic, Ube Latte, and Ube Milk.

Is Light Lavender a separate flavor?+

No. Light Lavender identifies Ube Milk.

How do I prepare my chosen flavor?+

Follow the preparation instructions on your product’s actual packaging. The Euro Blend team can confirm the recommended method for each available flavor.

How can I enquire about ordering?+

Enquire through the official Euro Blend Facebook page for current stock, prices, payment arrangements and delivery details.

Do you accept business enquiries?+

Yes. For reseller and business enquiries, contact Euro Blend on its official Facebook page. Pricing, quantities and terms are confirmed directly.
